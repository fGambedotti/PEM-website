Stakeholder Information

This one-page timeline summarises what happens, when it happens, and what each stakeholder needs to do to participate in the workshop process.

Initial onboarding steps should be completed by Wednesday 6 May 2026.

Phase 1

April 2026
to 6 May 2026

Recruitment and onboarding

You receive the invitation and core participation documents. This stage confirms who will participate and ensures everyone has the same baseline project information before the survey starts.

What you need to do

  • Read the information sheet.
  • Read the consent form.
  • Reply to the invitation email to confirm participation.
  • Complete these steps by Wednesday 6 May 2026.

Phase 2

Wednesday
6 May 2026

Pre-workshop survey sent

A short survey is sent to gather your views on community energy priorities and transition challenges. These responses are used to shape the first modelling assumptions before the live workshop discussion.

What you need to do

  • Complete the survey when you receive it.
  • Allow around 10-15 minutes.
  • Submit by the deadline stated in the survey email.

Phase 3

Wednesday
3 June 2026
2:00-4:00 PM UK

First participatory workshop

This is the core co-design session where participants discuss practical constraints, priorities, and trade-offs. Workshop insights are translated into model parameters so practitioner knowledge directly shapes the analysis.

What you need to do

This information will be provided at a later date.

Phase 4

June to September 2026

Scenario modelling and analysis

The research team encodes workshop inputs and runs multiple policy scenarios. This stage turns workshop discussions into comparable model outputs across equity, flexibility, and demand outcomes.

What you need to do

Phase 5

September to October 2026

Second participatory workshop (results feedback)

Preliminary findings are shared back with stakeholders for validation and challenge. This ensures interpretation is grounded in real-world practice before final reporting.
